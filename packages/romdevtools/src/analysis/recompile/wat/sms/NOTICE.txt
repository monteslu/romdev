Commit:    162c3439a697ffdfa00a5d3f44af103bf671f5dc (scripts/versions.json, cores.genesis_plus_gx)

Also:      blip_buf (Shay Green, as modified for Genesis Plus GX),
           LGPL-2.1-or-later. Text: LICENSES/LGPL-2.1.txt.

What is derived:
  hw.js          the VDP, I/O and frame order of genesis_plus_gx
  psg.js         core/sound/psg.c (SN76489), core/sound/blip_buf.c and the
                 frame hand-off of sound.c / system.c (also used by the
                 Genesis backend)
  cycles.js      Z80 timings checked against genesis_plus_gx's cc tables
  gpgx-hcounter.json, gpgx-sms-db.json, gpgx-sound.json
                 data extracted from genesis_plus_gx's source
The Z80 lowering (z80.js, interp.js) is romdev's own.

Every cart this backend builds contains that code: a recompiled SMS or Game
Gear cart may not be sold or used commercially, and its blip_buf part is
under the LGPL. See NOTICE at the package root.
