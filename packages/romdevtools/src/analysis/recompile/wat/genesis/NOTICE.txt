What is derived:
  gpgx-derived.json.gz  parsed ASTs of Musashi's opcode handlers (m68kops.h)
                        and z80.c's handlers, plus gpgx's tables, generated by
                        scripts/recompile-gen/genesis-gpgx.mjs
  ctrans.js, interp.js  evaluate those handlers into the cart's code
  runtime.js            gpgx's memory map, VDP, renderer, frame loop
  ym2612.js             core/sound/ym2612.c and sound.c's MD glue
  svp.js, gpgx-svp.json the SVP chip compiled from svp.c + ssp16.c
  gpgx-data.js, gpgx-md-db.json   loaders and data from gpgx's source
Sound also uses ../sms/psg.js (PSG + blip_buf).

Every cart this backend builds contains that code: a recompiled Genesis cart
may not be sold or used commercially, carries Musashi's notice, and its
blip_buf part is under the LGPL. See NOTICE at the package root.
