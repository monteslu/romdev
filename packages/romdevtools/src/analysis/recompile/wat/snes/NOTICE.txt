What is derived:
  memmap.js, loader.js, romfixes.js   snes9x memmap.cpp (maps, ROM loading,
                                      per-title fixes)
  cpu-wat.js, frame-wat.js            snes9x's CPU timing, DMA/HDMA, frame
  ppu-wat.js                          snes9x ppu.cpp, gfx.cpp, tileimpl.h
  apu-wat.js, snes9x-apu.json         the SPC700 (snes9x bapu smp) and the
                                      S-DSP; the 64-byte boot ROM in
                                      snes9x-apu.json is romdev's own
                                      clean-room code (scripts/spc700-ipl,
                                      MIT), not snes9x's
  sa1.js, superfx.js, sdd1.js, cx4.js, spc7110.js, bsx.js, msu1.js,
  chips-snes9x.js, chip-unit.js, snes9x-chips.json, snes9x-superfx.json
                                      snes9x's coprocessors, compiled from or
                                      ported from its source
  chips-ares.js, ares-chips.json      ares's chip cores (see NOTICE)
  hle-data.js                         romdev's own: the DSP-1, DSP-3 and ST010
                                      data ROM tables are not in
                                      snes9x-chips.json; a cart is built with
                                      the user's data ROM (options.firmware)
  snes9x-tables.json, snes9x-romfixes.json   data from snes9x's source
The 65816 lowering (lower.js) and layout.js are romdev's own.

Every cart this backend builds contains snes9x code: a recompiled SNES cart
may not be sold or used commercially, carries snes9x's licence text, and its
S-DSP part is under the LGPL. See NOTICE at the package root.
